# Blurred aerial background (for policy posts)

Campaign-supplied aerial photo of Melbourne's northern suburbs (2000×1497), upscaled 2× with Lanczos
(the blur makes AI upscaling unnecessary) and Gaussian-blurred at two strengths:

- `soft` (radius 10 at 4000px): suburb, freeway and parks still readable; best when there's little text.
- `strong` (radius 26 at 4000px): soft colour texture; best behind a full policy explainer.

Each comes as the full landscape frame (4000×2994), a 4:5 portrait crop at 2160×2700 (2× the 1080×1350
cards) and a square crop at 2160×2160. Crops are centred.

Check the photo's usage rights before publishing.
